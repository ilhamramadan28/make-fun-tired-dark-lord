A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.
The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections —
the flooded road nobody mended, generals waiting three days, a prophecy
that always needs one more moon — never in a plain statement of weariness.
The speaker is a specific dark lord: a map of taken kingdoms, a biting
third step, a throne room with new carpet in the eastern wing.

`main.py` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (exactly 100 words,
530 characters, mode 0644) and refuses to run if the word count, the
character gate, or the no-announced-tiredness rule is violated.
